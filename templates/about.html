About Standard Human Capital

Our History

Founded in 1994, Standard Human Capital began by providing high-level talent search services for Fortune 500 companies entering and operating in China. Over the years, we have grown into a human capital company with rich experience and outstanding strength in cross-border talent services, specializing in the finance, technology, and fintech industries.

Our Commitment to Globalization & Talent Solutions

✔ Foreign Enterprise Localization

We help foreign enterprises in China find local talent, achieve true localization, and seize the enormous opportunities present in the Asian market.

✔ Asian Enterprise Globalization

We bring in talent with international perspectives and practical experience for domestic enterprises, enabling Asian companies to implement global strategies and navigate cross-border expansion.

✔ Unicorn Company Business Partnerships

We act as strategic business partners for startups, accelerating their growth and creating value to help them become unicorns for the future.
