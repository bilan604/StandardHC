About Standard Human Capital

Summary

Founded in 1994, Standard Human Capital began by providing high-level talent search services for Fortune 500 companies entering and operating in China. Over the years, we have grown into a human capital company with rich experience and outstanding strength in cross-border talent services, specializing in the finance, technology, and fintech industries.

Our Commitment to Globalization & Talent Solutions

✔ Foreign Enterprise Localization

We help foreign enterprises in China find local talent, achieve true localization, and seize the enormous opportunities present in the Asian market.

✔ Asian Enterprise Globalization

We bring in talent with international perspectives and practical experience for domestic enterprises, enabling Asian companies to implement global strategies and navigate cross-border expansion.

✔ Unicorn Company Business Partnerships

We act as strategic business partners for startups, accelerating their growth and creating value to help them become unicorns for the future.

Corporate History

Early PRC Period - Localization and Talent Search

In the early days of China's reform and opening up, we focused on helping Fortune 500 companies find local talent for their China operations. This period was marked by significant contributions to the localization of foreign enterprises in China.

2000s - Supporting Chinese Companies Going Global

As Chinese companies began their international expansion, we pivoted to assist them in finding international talent. Our expertise in cross-cultural recruitment became invaluable for companies seeking to establish a global presence.

2010s - Tech Industry Focus

With the rise of China's tech sector, we specialized in recruiting for technology and fintech companies. Our deep understanding of both traditional and emerging industries allowed us to bridge talent gaps effectively.

Present Day - Global Human Capital Solutions

Today, we operate as a comprehensive human capital solutions provider, serving both multinational corporations and emerging unicorns. Our services span executive search, talent acquisition, and strategic HR consulting across global markets.
