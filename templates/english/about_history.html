Development History

As one of the earliest established professional human resources companies in China, we began operations in mainland China as early as 1994, providing mid-to-senior level talent search services for mainly Fortune 500 companies.

We were the first to establish a complete service network in China’s major cities: started national network setup in 1996 with offices in Beijing, Shanghai, Hong Kong, Chengdu, and Wuhan.

We were the first to implement industry segmentation strategy - the first to develop an HR database.

In 2000, partnered with HP to establish China’s first HP-authorized software R&D center.

Our predecessor, Standard Guangzhou Company, established a Sino-foreign joint venture with a Fortune 500 HR enterprise in 2003 and became the largest human resource service provider in China.

In 2009, we focused on providing in-depth integrated financial and accounting recruitment and outsourcing services for clients in the financial sector (banking, insurance, securities, funds, trusts, real estate investment & financing, TMT, and finance) and listed companies; by combining our experience with deep insights into the Chinese market, we are better positioned to help clients plan their workforce. In China, we are proud to provide clients with comprehensive HR services.
