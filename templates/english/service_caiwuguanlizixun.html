Wealth Management

Program Structure
A series of training modules have been designed, dividing the workflow into four major phases: Initial Contact, Understanding the Client, Sales Presentation, and After-Sales Service. The goal is to shift the marketing mindset from sales-oriented to a balanced focus on both marketing and asset quality, enhancing branch service and marketing capabilities while reducing operational risks.


 
Expected Outcomes
Through this training, we aim to leverage the bank’s existing resources and culture. With instructor coaching, demonstrations, observation, feedback, repeated role-play, and hands-on practice, we will reinforce the consultative sales process and refine sales techniques. We assist relationship managers in establishing correct marketing concepts and developing good work habits.
During the coaching process, participants are guided through reflective questioning, enabling them to analyze problems independently, propose solutions, draft action plans, internalize asset allocation concepts, and deepen the demand-driven sales mindset. The goal is a win-win-win: increased client loyalty, improved bank performance, and individual growth.
 
Action Plan
Unlike traditional classroom training that often lacks practicality and applicability, this program uses on-site branch coaching to provide hands-on support and guidance. This narrows the gap between theory and practice, bringing training closer to the daily work of relationship managers and ensuring effective application.

Pre-Project Research
To ensure the training content and objectives align with the bank’s needs, we will conduct preliminary research on both the head office and participating branches before project launch. The purpose is to explain training content, communicate objectives, and establish mutual understanding. This is done primarily through on-site interviews and supplemented by surveys.

Certification
To engage the branch in the learning outcomes of participants, a certification session can be held at the end of the program. A chief examiner from the branch will be invited to participate in the assessment process.
