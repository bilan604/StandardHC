Global Human Capital Solutions

Strategic Talent Solutions

Standard Human Capital is a human capital company specializing in cross-border talent services. We partner with established enterprises, helping them scale through strategic talent acquisition, business expansion, and localization solutions.

Core Business

Executive Search

We provide specialized recruitment focused on identifying, attracting, and hiring top-level executives, senior managers, and other high-impact people for global businesses.

  • We proactively seek out highly qualified candidates who may not be actively looking for a job.
  • We provide complete confidentiality for sensitive leadership transitions, ensuring discretion for both clients and candidates.
  • We utilize industry expertise in sectors such as technology and finance to identify the most suitable leaders quickly for clients.

Talent Acquisition

We conduct comprehensive and in-depth searches, screening precisely and recommending potential candidates for target roles.

  • We integrate with business operations to accurately grasp job requirements.
  • We thoroughly research the talent distribution channels of target markets
  • We leverage multiple public and proprietary professional channels to find candidates of the best fit.

Impact

We help foreign enterprises localize into the Asian market, local enterprise globalize into foreign markets, and unicorns accelerate their growth through executive search and talent acquisition.

  • We focus on on placing leaders who will drive growth, innovation, and transformation within the organization.
  • We conduct in-depth assessments and reference checks to ensure the best cultural and strategic fit.
